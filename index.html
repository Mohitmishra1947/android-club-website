WHO WE ARE

A Community Driven by Innovation.

Founded in 2017 by Abhay Nigam, Android Club at VIT Bhopal has grown into a community focused on innovation, learning, and Android development.

Through technical workshops, collaborative activities, and a shared passion for technology, the club creates an environment where students can explore their creativity and strengthen their development skills.

We believe that learning becomes more meaningful when ideas are shared, challenges are explored, and students build together.
